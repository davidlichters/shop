The feel

What does it feel like?

Snug, cozy and calming to lie in, like a hug. How it feels is personal: some people love being wrapped and some don't, so you get 30 days to find out.

What if it feels too tight?

Being wrapped can feel new, so start on the couch with it pulled up to your chest. Still too snug? The larger size gives more room. If it is not for you at all, the guarantee covers you: full refund, and you keep it.

Will it slide down in the night?

It stretches with you when you turn, so there is no loose edge to pull away. Some restless sleepers do find it creeping down, and you pull it back up. A snugger size holds closer, and if it keeps slipping, the guarantee covers it.

Can I use it on top of my usual bedding?

Yes. It works on any bed, on the couch, or on top of your usual bedding.

Is it the same as a weighted blanket?

No. The snug feel comes from stretch fabric around you, not from filling on top of you.

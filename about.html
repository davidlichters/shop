About Tuckwell

Tuckwell started with a small, stubborn annoyance: the blanket that is never where you left it. It slides off one side, it bunches at your feet, and at some point in the night you are fishing for it on the floor. We thought there should be a better answer than a bigger blanket.

So we built this store around one thing, and we would rather do one thing properly than fill a page with a hundred. The Tuckwell Cocoon is a single piece of stretchy fabric that wraps you from your shoulders to your toes. There is nothing to tuck in, because you are already inside it. It feels snug and cozy, like a hug.

What we care about

We are a small, independent shop, and we say so because it shapes how we work. There is no call center and no script. When you write to us, a person reads your message and answers it. We keep our promises short so that we can keep them: we tell you what the product is, what it is not, and what happens if it is not right for you.

That includes being plain about what the Cocoon does. It is made for comfort. It is not a medical product and it does not treat anything. It comes in adult and children's sizes.

What we sell

We focus on comfort at home: simple things that make the end of your day better, chosen one at a time. If a product here ever lets you down, that is our problem to fix and not yours.

Our promise to you

Every order comes with our 30-day money-back guarantee. If the Cocoon is not for you, email us within 30 days of delivery and we refund you in full. You do not send anything back. If something arrives damaged or wrong, we replace it or refund it, whichever you prefer. We answer every email within one business day, Monday to Friday.

Getting it to you

We ship to addresses in the United States. Your order is packed and handed to our shipping partner, and you get a tracking number by email so you can follow it the whole way. Most orders arrive 7 to 12 business days after they ship. If something takes longer than it should, we will tell you before you have to ask.

Say hello

If you have a question before you order, or you are not sure which size is right for you, write to us at tuckwell@lichters.email. We would rather help you pick the right one than have you guess.

Returns & Refunds

Policy Overview

We want you to feel safe buying from Tuckwell. Every order is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee, there is no restocking fee, and you do not need to send anything back. If anything is unclear, write to tuckwell@lichters.email and a person will answer within one business day.

Return & Exchange Eligibility

Any order placed directly in our store is covered. You do not need to give us a reason, and the product does not need to be unused. Because the Cocoon is something you wear to bed, we do not resell returned items, which is why we let you keep it. One refund per household.

Return Window

You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered. The date in your tracking information counts.

Return Shipping Responsibility

For a money-back refund there is nothing to ship: you keep the item and pay nothing. If an item arrives damaged, defective or wrong, we cover everything.

How to Start a Return or Exchange

Email tuckwell@lichters.email with your order number and a photo of the item. We confirm your request within one business day. That is the whole process: no forms, no labels, no trip to the post office.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We send a free replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer.

Cancellations

If you change your mind before your order has shipped, email us and we cancel it and refund you in full. Once it has shipped we cannot stop it, but the 30-day guarantee still covers you.

Refund Processing Time

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Your bank may need a few more days to show it.

Our Error vs. Customer-Initiated Returns

If the mistake is ours, such as a wrong size or a damaged item, we make it right at no cost to you. If you simply decide the Cocoon is not for you, the guarantee covers that too. In both cases the price you paid comes back to you in full.

Exchanges Process

If you ordered the wrong size, email us within 30 days of delivery. We refund the item under the guarantee and you can order the size you need. You keep the first one.

Our Promise

We would rather refund an order than keep a customer who is unhappy. If something is not right, write to us first. We reply within one business day and solve most cases in a single email.
